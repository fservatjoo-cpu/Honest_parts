GOOD TO KNOW

Is it really free?
Yes, for a short time while we launch. Later, Quote Check will be a small paid service, and this page will show the price before you send anything.
Will you tell me my mechanic is cheating me?
No. A higher price isn't proof of anything. Shops have different costs, warranties, and part brands. We show you the numbers and give you fair questions to ask, so you can talk with your mechanic with confidence.
Can you diagnose my car from the quote?
No. We don't see or test your car, so we can't confirm what's wrong. Our report is about prices, normal labor time, and priorities. For anything about brakes, steering, or tires, safety comes first: if the shop says it's unsafe, take that seriously.
What do you do with my quote?
We use it only to write your report. We never share it or post it. See our Privacy Policy.
