GOOD TO KNOW

What does it cost?
Sending a request is free. The quote is one total price: the part, shipping to your ZIP code, sales tax, and our finder's fee. No extra charges after that.
What's in the quote?
The part's condition, mileage when we know it, the seller's warranty and return terms, and the delivery time. Read these before you pay, because they come from the seller and are different for each part.
How do you know it fits?
We match the part to your VIN before we quote. If we can't confirm the fit, we tell you and don't quote it.
Returns and refunds
Tell us within 10 days of delivery if you want to return a part. If it arrives defective, or it isn't the part we quoted, we arrange a replacement or a refund under the seller's warranty. If it doesn't fit because the VIN or details you sent were wrong, or you change your mind, return shipping and the seller's restocking fee (usually 15% of the part price) come out of the refund. Refunds are sent once the seller receives the part back.
Warranty
Used-part warranties usually cover the part only, not labor. Many are valid only if the part is installed by a licensed mechanic, so a do-it-yourself install can void it. Your quote shows the exact warranty for your part.
What if you can't find it?
We email you to say so. You owe nothing.
Parts we don't source
Airbags, seat belt parts, and catalytic converters. For safety and legal reasons, buy these new from a parts store or dealer.
